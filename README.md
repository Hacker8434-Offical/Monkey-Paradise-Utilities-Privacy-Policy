# Monkey Paradise Utilities – Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: November 14, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Monkey Paradise Utilities (“the Bot,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you interact with the Bot or use its features within the Monkey Paradise Discord community and Minecraft server.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is legally required, it will be obtained as required by applicable law.

## 1. Information We Collect

The Bot may collect and process information necessary to operate its features, depending on which systems are enabled.

### Discord Information

The Bot may store or process:

- Discord User IDs
- Discord Server (Guild) IDs
- Channel IDs and Role IDs
- Message IDs, where required
- Server configuration and utility settings
- Welcome and farewell event information
- Command usage and relevant interaction records

### Minecraft and Economy Information

To operate the Monkey Paradise economy system and related Minecraft features, the Bot may store:

- Minecraft usernames and UUIDs, if account linking is enabled
- Player account identifiers
- Virtual currency balances
- Currency earned and spent
- Economy transaction records
- Rewards, bonuses, and purchase history
- Player statistics associated with supported features

This information is used to maintain player balances, process transactions, and support the server's economy.

### Minigame Information

If minigames are enabled, the Bot may collect or store:

- Game participation records
- Game scores and results
- Wins and losses
- Player rankings and leaderboards
- Rewards earned through minigames
- Game-related statistics and progress

The specific information stored depends on the minigames and features implemented.

### Welcome and General Utility Information

The Bot may process information required for basic community features, including:

- Member join and leave events
- Discord usernames and user identifiers
- Welcome message configurations
- Configured announcement channels
- Utility command settings
- Relevant command results and error logs

These features are intended to support the operation and organization of the Monkey Paradise community.

### Technical and Diagnostic Information

The Bot may record:

- Commands used and their execution status
- Relevant user and server identifiers
- Error and diagnostic logs
- System performance information
- Information necessary to investigate bugs, suspicious activity, or abuse

The information actually collected depends on the Bot's implementation and configuration.

## 2. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

The Bot is not designed to collect the following information as part of its ordinary operation:

- Discord or Minecraft account passwords
- Credit card or banking information
- Government-issued identification
- Private messages that the Bot has not been permitted or configured to access
- Email addresses or phone numbers, unless a separately disclosed feature requires them

The Bot may process message content when necessary for a specifically enabled feature. Ordinary welcome messages, economy commands, and minigames do not inherently require access to private conversations.

Please do not submit passwords, authentication tokens, or unnecessary sensitive information through Bot commands or community channels.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected by the Bot may be used to:

- Operate and maintain the Monkey Paradise economy system
- Track virtual currency balances and transactions
- Process purchases, rewards, and economy-related commands
- Run minigames and record their results
- Maintain leaderboards and player statistics
- Send welcome and farewell messages
- Provide general utility commands
- Configure and maintain community features
- Prevent currency abuse, cheating, spam, and exploitation
- Investigate technical problems and incorrect transactions
- Improve the reliability and security of the Bot

Information is not intended to be used for advertising or marketing purposes.

## 4. Virtual Economy and Transaction Records

The Bot may maintain virtual currency balances and transaction histories for players using the Monkey Paradise economy system.

These records may include currency earned, spent, transferred, or awarded through supported features.

Virtual currency managed by the Bot is intended for use within the Monkey Paradise community and its supported systems. Unless explicitly stated otherwise by the server administration, virtual currency has no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for real money.

Economy records may be retained to:

- Maintain accurate player balances
- Investigate disputed transactions
- Correct technical errors
- Detect duplication exploits or unauthorized currency generation
- Prevent fraudulent activity
- Maintain the integrity of the economy

Authorized administrators may review relevant economy records when investigating issues or maintaining the system.

Attempts to exploit economy commands, manipulate balances, duplicate rewards, or interfere with transaction processing may result in corrective actions or community moderation.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information for commercial purposes.

Information may be accessed or disclosed when reasonably necessary for:

**Community administration:** Authorized Monkey Paradise staff may review economy records, minigame statistics, configuration settings, and relevant logs.

**Player-facing features:** Certain information, such as currency balances, scores, rankings, or leaderboard positions, may be displayed to other community members through Bot commands or public channels.

**Security and abuse prevention:** Relevant records may be reviewed to investigate cheating, suspicious transactions, exploitation, or violations of community rules.

**Service providers:** Information may be processed by hosting providers, database services, or external APIs used to operate the Bot, where applicable.

**Legal obligations:** Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or in response to valid legal requests.

Access to stored information should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Bot, maintain its features, and protect the Monkey Paradise community.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information.

For example:

- Economy balances may be retained while an account remains active within the system.
- Transaction records may be retained to investigate disputes, errors, or abuse.
- Minigame statistics may be retained to maintain leaderboards and player progress.
- Welcome and member event records may be retained where necessary for system operation.
- Diagnostic logs may be retained to troubleshoot issues and protect system security.

Where practical and appropriate, information may be deleted when it is no longer needed or following a valid request.

Some information may need to be retained for a reasonable period for security, dispute resolution, system integrity, or legal compliance.

Deleting or leaving a Discord server does not necessarily result in the immediate deletion of all associated economy records or other stored information.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the Bot against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Depending on the infrastructure used, safeguards may include access restrictions, administrative permissions, and appropriate protections for stored information.

However, no online service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Users should report suspected bugs, unauthorized transactions, or other security concerns to the Monkey Paradise staff.

## 8. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and may interact with additional services needed for its functionality.

These may include:

- Discord
- Minecraft server software and related services
- Hosting providers
- Database providers
- External APIs or other enabled integrations

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

Monkey Paradise does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not specifically directed toward children below the minimum age permitted to use Discord under applicable requirements.

Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and any other applicable age-related restrictions.

We do not intentionally collect personal information in violation of applicable age-related privacy laws.

If you believe information has been collected inappropriately, contact the Monkey Paradise staff so the matter can be reviewed.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

- Request information about personal data associated with you
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of personal information where applicable
- Ask how your information is collected and used
- Request restrictions on certain processing where applicable
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

Requests may be subject to verification and applicable legal limitations. Some information, including records needed to maintain economy integrity, investigate abuse, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations, may need to be retained where legally permitted.

To submit a privacy request, contact the Monkey Paradise staff through the official Discord server. Do not share passwords or other confidential credentials when making a request.

## 11. Responsibilities of Server Administrators

Monkey Paradise administrators are responsible for configuring and managing the Bot's features appropriately.

This may include:

- Configuring economy settings and rewards
- Managing access to administrative commands
- Ensuring economy records are handled responsibly
- Configuring welcome messages and utility features
- Managing minigames, scores, and leaderboards
- Restricting access to sensitive logs and records
- Informing community members about relevant data practices where required

Administrative access should only be granted to individuals who need it to manage the community or operate the Bot.

## 12. Cookies and Website Tracking

Monkey Paradise Utilities does not use browser cookies as part of its ordinary Discord bot functionality.

If a separate website, dashboard, or web-based service is introduced, it may have its own cookie or tracking practices, which will be disclosed where appropriate.

Updated versions will be published through an appropriate Monkey Paradise community channel or other designated location.

The effective date will be updated when appropriate. Where required by law, users will be notified of significant changes or asked to provide consent.

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact the Monkey Paradise staff through the official Monkey Paradise Discord server.

Please provide enough information for staff to review your request without sharing unnecessary personal or confidential information.
